## Description

A lexical retrieval set in German, French and Italian, beside `dataset.json` (English) and `dataset_zh.json` (Chinese). It measures the word path within a language with no embedder, and holds each category's current numbers as floors.

### The set
`pkg/eval/testdata/dataset_ch.json`, loaded by `eval.BuiltinSwiss()`, in the format of the two existing sets:
- **Source**: the Swiss Federal Constitution (SR 101), Fedlex (fedlex.admin.ch), version of 3 March 2024, in its four official languages. The source is named in the dataset's `description` and in the test.
- **Documents**: 24 articles × 4 languages (de, fr, it, rm), 96 documents. `id` is `<article>__<lang>`, `group` is the article. The 24 are the shortest articles among those the questions ask about (69 to 513 characters).
- **Queries**: 230, each asked against the article in its own language. Romansh texts are distractors only.
  - `plain` (72): a question and its two translations, as written.
  - `unaccented` (49): the same questions with their accents removed, when that changes them, as people type them.
  - `heading` (72): the article's heading in each language, as a short query ("Liberté économique").
  - `heading_unaccented` (31): the same headings without accents ("Liberte economique").
  - `compound` (6): German questions whose word the article holds only inside a compound ("Versicherung" in "Unfallversicherung").
- **Negatives**: 4, about things the Constitution does not hold (a recipe, a ski pass). All come back empty.
- The questions and negatives are written by a model (Claude), not by native speakers. The legal texts are the official ones.

### The test
`TestSwissLexicalRetrievalQuality` (`pkg/eval/eval_ch_test.go`) follows `TestChineseLexicalRetrievalQuality`: SQLite, no embedder, `SearchKnowledge` in lexical mode, `top_k` 10. It runs `eval.Run` once per category and holds a floor on recall@10 and MRR for each, a little under today's value:

| Category | n | recall@10 | MRR | Floor (recall@10, MRR) |
| :- | -: | -: | -: | :- |
| plain | 72 | 0.986 | 0.926 | 0.95, 0.90 |
| unaccented | 49 | 0.980 | 0.908 | 0.95, 0.88 |
| heading | 72 | 0.764 | 0.698 | 0.74, 0.67 |
| heading_unaccented | 31 | 0.387 | 0.349 | 0.35, 0.32 |
| compound | 6 | 0.833 | 0.524 | 0.80, 0.50 |

`heading_unaccented` is expected to be low today. FTS5 `unicode61` folds accents, so the word index finds "Liberté économique" for "Liberte economique". The coverage floor of v2.138.0 then compares the words with their accents, finds none of the query in the row, and drops it. Its floor holds the measured value; #gc-25 (the coverage floor compares words without accents) raises it to 0.806 and 0.769.

`compound` measures something the word index does not do (a word inside a compound). Six queries only, so it is a marker, not a score.

### Scope
No code change outside `pkg/eval`. The format of `eval.Query` has no field for alternate queries, so the cross-language questions (a question in one language, the article in another) are not in this set.

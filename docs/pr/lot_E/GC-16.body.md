## Description

Stacked on GC-15 (entities under a caller scope), itself on GC-14 (documents and memories under a caller scope); the diff below is the one commit on top of GC-15.

A confined key may not call any graph tool today: the allowlist refuses them, because their answers reached every collection. With GC-14 and GC-15 the facade holds what they serve to the caller scope. This PR opens the seven tools that walk the graph from a seed or read nodes by id. Touches `pkg/authz/allowlist.go` and tests in `pkg/authz`, `pkg/rpcserver` and `pkg/httpapi`.

### Fix

- The seven tools enter `confinedTools` with both stores, no scope field and no gate, and a new mark, `facade`: the facade confines every store they touch to the caller scope (`cortexdb.CallerScope`, set by `Key.CallerContext`), so a key confined on any field may call them, and `AuthorizeToolArgs` asks for no gate.
- They have no `collection` argument to force. Their node ids and entity names are data, as `plan.filters.document_ids` is: a node the scope hides answers as a node that does not exist (GC-14, GC-15), so loading it in the guard and refusing it would tell the two apart.
- `ConfinedToolsTable` shows them as confined "by the facade (CallerScope)".
- The tools that read the whole stored graph without a walk stay refused, now each with its reason: `graph_list_all`, `graph_cypher_query`, `rank_graph_nodes`, `predict_graph_edges`, `disambiguate_mentions`, `graph_statistics`.
- `find_nodes` stays refused too, with its reason: it scans the content of every node and passes no hidden-node filter. Once gc-13 ("find_nodes leaves out the hidden nodes") is merged, it inherits GC-14 and GC-15 and can move to this list with one line, its arguments `names`, `node_types`, `limit`.

## Performance Impact
None in the guard: the seven tools name no row it loads. The cost of the facade checks is in GC-14 and GC-15.

## Breaking Changes
None for an unconfined key. A confined key may now call the seven tools, and is served what its scope reaches.
